Fix Microphone Permission Blocked by Edge
🔴 If you see "Blocked" next to Microphone in Site Settings:
  1. In Edge Address Bar:
    • Look for the ⓘ icon (info) or 🔒 icon to the left of URL
    • If you see "Microphone: Blocked", click on it
    • Change from "Block" to "Allow"
    • Close the popup
  2. Or use Settings Menu:
    • Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete (or click ⋯ → Settings)
    • Go to Privacy, search, and services
    • Scroll to "Site permissions"
    • Click "Microphone"
    • Look for "10.138.248.168" or this site in the list
    • If it's in "Block" list, change it to "Allow"
    • If it's not there, add it manually
  3. Windows Settings Check:
    • Press Windows + I to open Settings
    • Go to Privacy & Security → Microphone
    • Make sure "Microphone access" is ON
    • Scroll down and find "Microsoft Edge"
    • Make sure it shows "On"
✅ After Enabling Permissions:
  1. Refresh the page: Press Ctrl + R or F5
  2. Try again: Click "Start Listening"
  3. Grant permission: When prompted, click "Allow"
Note: After changing settings, you may need to close and reopen Edge completely for changes to take effect.
